About me

I'm Shoaib.

A Web & UI/UX Designer based in Hyderabad, Pakistan — with over 3 years of experience designing interfaces and websites that are clear, purposeful, and easy to use.

I work across the full design process: from early discovery and wireframing through to polished, ready-to-build Figma screens. My projects have spanned SaaS platforms, service businesses, e-learning sites, and booking experiences — which means I've learned to adapt quickly to different audiences and goals, rather than applying the same visual template to every problem.

I take freelance projects through Fiverr and Upwork, and I care a lot about making the collaboration process straightforward — clear scope, realistic timelines, and no design jargon that gets in the way of the actual work.

How I work

A process that keeps clients in the loop.

I keep the process lightweight and transparent — no unnecessary back-and-forth, just a clear path from brief to finished design.

01

Discovery & brief

Understanding your goals, audience, and what success looks like before any design begins.

02

Wireframes

Low-fidelity layouts that nail the structure and flow before we commit to a visual direction.

03

High-fidelity design

Full Figma screens with real typography, color, and spacing — ready for review and feedback.

04

Handoff

A clean, annotated Figma file your developer can open and build from without guessing.
